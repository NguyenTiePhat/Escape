# Tension, adaptive audio, visual effects and scares

Installed in School_Test under `Tension and scares`. The user chose to retain Built-in rendering; no URP migration or Volume package was added.

- TensionManager publishes OnTensionChanged(float), clamps to 0–1 and uses exponential-factor Lerp (rise 2/s, fall 0.3/s). Input comes from the nearest active monster within a 25 m danger radius, monster state, player-owned NoiseManager events with decay, and a darkness estimate. Chase/Attack is maximum threat. Darkness uses authored MonsterDarknessZone regions, light probes and visible nearby point/spot lights, rather than screen luminance.
- AdaptiveMusic keeps three synchronized looping AudioSources running and interpolates their volumes. The supplied layers are generated seamless 16-second minor-key drones/pulses, editable through the component's AudioSources. No external music dependency is required.
- HeartbeatController starts below 12 m, schedules a short generated double pulse at 65–145 BPM, and adjusts gain with proximity. Environmental spatial sources receive mild low-pass filtering; original cutoff values are retained and restored. Music and heartbeat are excluded from this filter.
- TensionVisuals applies Built-in image effects: vignette, grain, subtle RGB separation and desaturation. Chase adds a small camera rotation offset, removed without overwriting look/death poses. The shader reference is serialized to keep it available in builds.
- Existing F2 Settings now includes a scrollable accessibility section: reduced effects, visual intensity, shake intensity, heartbeat gain, environmental muffling and scare toggle. Reduced mode disables visuals/shake/scares/muffling and reduces heartbeat gain to 10%; heartbeat can separately be set to zero. Preferences persist.
- ScareDirector owns three authored entries: a distant sound near the key-machine corridor, a nearby door closing after the player passes, and a silhouette at the end of a western corridor. Trigger points were projected onto the existing NavMesh. Entry, low tension and no active Chase/Attack are required. One global cooldown is clamped to at least 90 gameplay seconds. Event IDs can only run once per scene round; scene reload resets the roster. The existing Door collision checks govern slams.
- Won/Lost/dead/escaped player states suppress scares and fade adaptive audio; visual effects stop affecting the result camera.

Validation: `tension-check.txt` covers Play Mode logic, smoothing/events, player noise ownership, normalized music crossfades, continuous layers, 12 m threshold, compiled shader, reduced settings, cooldown/one-shot/chase suppression, active-shadow cancellation and round end. `tension-integration-check.txt` covers measured distance to a real monster, its actual Chase state, environmental low-pass restoration, and a complete collision-safe door slam. `tension-high.png` and `tension-reduced.png` were rendered from the same scene camera and visually inspected. Audio scheduling/gain was checked programmatically; a subjective listening/mix pass was not performed.

Reusable setup/validation scripts are in Assets/Recovery/Editor. TensionSetup.Run installs once and starts validation; it refuses a duplicate root. TensionValidation.Integration runs the focused integration checks without saving test mutations. These explicit batch entry points exit Unity on completion. Backup: School_Test-before-tension.unity.
